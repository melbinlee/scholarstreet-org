Scholar Street

Organized to operate as a Scholarship Granting Organization under the federal Education Freedom Tax Credit (IRC §25F). We award K–12 scholarships to income-eligible students in the states we serve, on the basis of need and merit.

Updates on Scholar Street and the federal tax credit. We’ll also let you know when scholarship applications open.

Contributions may qualify for the Education Freedom Tax Credit under IRC §25F beginning January 1, 2027. Provided for general informational purposes only — not tax, legal, or financial advice. Consult a qualified tax professional regarding individual eligibility.
