How it works

Three portals. One platform.
Everything connected.

Scholarship Insights connects donors, administrators, and families through a single compliance-first system built around IRC §25F.

01
◈
Donors are managed by the SGO
SGO administrators manage all donor records, track credit amounts, and generate §25F acknowledgment letters. Donors interact with their SGO directly — Scholarship Insights handles the compliance layer behind the scenes.
02
⬡
SGOs operate compliantly
Administrators manage fund-segregated pools per state, enforce the priority queue, verify AMI eligibility, and track the 90% ratio — all on one dashboard with a complete immutable audit trail.
03
◆
Families apply and receive
The family portal includes AMI eligibility calculation, income verification, multi-student applications, scholarship status, and IRC §139K tax-free award letters.
