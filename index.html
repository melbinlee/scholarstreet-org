Why Now

A new federal opportunity
for scholarship giving.

In 2025, Congress created the first federal tax credit for K–12 scholarship donations — the Education Freedom Tax Credit (IRC §25F) — effective January 1, 2027. Scholar Street is registered to operate in multiple states and is building its footprint ahead of the January 1, 2027 launch.

$1,700
Per Taxpayer

A dollar-for-dollar federal credit, nonrefundable, with a five-year carryforward. Joint-filer treatment is pending Treasury guidance.

Any Taxpayer
No Income Limit

Any individual with U.S. federal tax liability can claim the credit. There is no donor income limit. Cash gifts only.

300% AMI
Access, Not Cost

Eligibility reaches households up to 300% of area median income. For many families, cost — not eligibility — stands between their child and the right school.

Our Mission

Scholarships built on
need and merit.

Scholar Street is organized to operate as a Scholarship Granting Organization under the Education Freedom Tax Credit program. We award K–12 scholarships to income-eligible students in the states we serve based on both financial need and academic merit — evaluated together for every award.

Reach Underserved Communities

Donors may support our general fund or direct their gift to a designated fund serving a specific community — faith-based education, special needs, or military families.

Awards Sized to Matter

A $5,000 target award, scaling with need and funding. We would rather fund fewer students than spread support too thin to change a family’s decision.

Built to Scale

This tax credit only reaches as many children as SGOs can efficiently serve. Our platform is built to scale nationally as the federal program expands state by state.
