Leadership

The people behind
Scholar Street.

Two co-founders who understand every child is unique, driven by a single focused mission: helping families provide the best education for their child.

Ryan Hill
Co-Founder, Board Chair,
President & CEO

As Board Chair, President & CEO, Ryan drives Scholar Street’s vision, donor development, and government relations, and serves as the organization’s primary spokesperson.

He brings more than 20 years of capital markets and business development experience, including executive leadership roles as Managing Director at a structured finance firm and at an early-stage fintech company. This blend of institutional capital experience and start-up operating discipline is the same foundation Scholar Street is built on. Most recently, Ryan served as SVP of Capital Markets at Student Quarters, leading equity and debt strategy for a $1.3B CRE asset portfolio. He holds a BBA from James Madison University and an MBA from Georgetown’s McDonough School of Business.

“A value-based education matters to our family — our son attends Saint Agnes School in Arlington. Through Scholar Street, I hope to help families pursue the education that’s right for their child by lessening financial obstacles.”

ryan@scholarstreet.org
Mel Sisaithong
Co-Founder, COO,
Secretary & Treasurer

As COO, Secretary & Treasurer, Mel is Scholar Street’s operational backbone — overseeing technology, compliance, financial management, and recordkeeping to meet the standards of an IRS-designated Scholarship Granting Organization.

After more than 15 years in wine importing and distribution, Mel’s path changed following the loss of his son, Milo, to a congenital heart defect — redirecting his career toward healthcare and technology. Introduced to Salesforce at the United Network for Organ Sharing (UNOS), he earned his Administrator certification and became a Senior Salesforce Administrator across four organizations spanning healthcare, finance, and international development. He holds a BS from Virginia Commonwealth University.

“My daughter needed more than just a school — she needed a community; one that truly saw her for who she is. Every child deserves that fit.”

mel@scholarstreet.org
Scholar Street

Organized to operate as a Scholarship Granting Organization under the federal Education Freedom Tax Credit (IRC §25F). We award K–12 scholarships to income-eligible Virginia students on the basis of need and merit.

Updates on Scholar Street and the federal tax credit. We’ll also let you know when scholarship applications open.

Contributions may qualify for the Education Freedom Tax Credit under IRC §25F beginning January 1, 2027. Provided for general informational purposes only — not tax, legal, or financial advice. Consult a qualified tax professional regarding individual eligibility.
