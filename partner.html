Partner With Us

Built around your school. Built to make every gift count.

The Scholarship Insights platform we use is set up to align with our school and corporate partners, and it can be customized to the way you work. Your community gets a place to give. Your families get a place to apply. We handle the rest, at no cost to you.

Become a Partner How it works

Every partner receives

  • A dedicated donor giving page
  • A family scholarship application portal
  • A designated fund for your school
  • Fundraising support
  • Reporting and application services
No cost to our partners.

Schools and corporate partners pay nothing to join, to use the platform, or to receive our reporting and support. We keep administrative costs low, so the impact of your community's generosity reaches the students it is meant for.

Aligned with how you work

No two partners look alike. We set up the platform around your school or company, not the other way around.

K-12 schools

Give your parents, alumni and wider community one trusted place to learn about the Education Freedom Tax Credit and give, and give your eligible families a place to apply. The giving page is designed to sit on your own website, in your look and voice.

Corporate partners

Bring the opportunity to the people in your organization. A customized page and simple communications help employees and owners who owe federal tax learn about the credit and give. The credit is claimed by individual taxpayers.

Customized to you: your branding, your website, your communications calendar, your reporting needs.

How your school's fund works

Built for impact in your community, and designed so that no gift sits idle.

Gifts are identified to your school

Every gift made through your page carries your school's unique identifier, so your community's generosity is tracked and reported to you.

Your families apply on the same platform

Eligible students from your school apply through your partner portal while gifts come in.

An independent committee decides

The Independent Scholarship Selection Committee reviews applications and makes award decisions to maximize impact.

Nothing is left on the table

Surplus supports eligible students at other partner schools. If your families need more than your community has given, they can be supported by gifts made through other partner pages.

Beyond this year

Aligned for the long run: Scholars For the Future

A tax credit is a yearly opportunity. We want to build something that lasts longer. Scholars For the Future is an endowment Scholar Street is establishing from its own operating share, so that today's momentum becomes lasting support for students.

As it grows, its distributions are directed toward scholarships and the educational needs of the communities our partner schools serve. It is how we turn alignment with our partners into something we build alongside them, year after year.

Scholar Street

Organized to operate as a Scholarship Granting Organization under the federal Education Freedom Tax Credit (IRC §25F). We will award K–12 scholarships to income-eligible students in the states we serve, on the basis of need and merit.
