Governance & Compliance

Accountability is
not optional.

Organized to operate as a Scholarship Granting Organization under §25F, Scholar Street is built on a rigorous compliance framework. Families trust us with sensitive information. Donors trust us with their contributions. We honor both responsibilities.

The core safeguard

The people who raise the money do not award it

Scholarship recipients are selected by an Independent Scholarship Selection Committee that operates outside Scholar Street’s Board of Directors. The Board sets policy and raises funds. The Committee reviews applications and makes awards against published criteria: financial need and academic merit. Donors may designate the community their gift serves, but never the individual recipient.

Our compliance commitments
IRC §25F Federal Authorization

We operate as a Scholarship Granting Organization under the Education Freedom Tax Credit provisions of the Internal Revenue Code, including the federal requirement that at least 90% of income fund scholarships — measured separately for each state in which we are listed.

Income Verification & AMI Methodology

Income determination procedures consistent with current HUD Median Family Income data, with Treasury and IRS guidance under §25F monitored as it develops.

Application & Award Recordkeeping

Every application, eligibility determination, award decision, and disbursement is documented and retained in accordance with federal recordkeeping requirements.

Donor Acknowledgment Letters

Each donor receives a formal written §25F acknowledgment containing the information required to claim the Education Freedom Tax Credit on their federal return.

Non-Discrimination Policy

Scholarships are awarded without regard to race, religion, gender, national origin, or disability, and determined solely by published eligibility criteria.
